## Priority 1 — broken or wrong

| # | Finding | Evidence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | **Injury statuses `NA`, `COV`, `DNR` aren't handled.** The optimizer's skip rule (`OPT_SKIP` = Out/IR/Doubtful/PUP/Sus) lets these players start. `injuryChip` shows one letter, and `DNR` gets Doubtful's colour. | **Tested:** the live `injury_status` counts for players on a team were `NA` 4, `COV` 2, `DNR` 1. Optimizer behaviour with such a player is **inferred**. | Add them to the not-startable rule and give each a readable chip label/colour. Keep one shared list of statuses for the optimizer, the Playing card and the chips. |
| 1b | **The player database refresh is too heavy for phones.** `/players/nfl` is re-downloaded every open or foreground on game days (at most every 15 min) and parsed in the browser. | **Tested:** 14,659,782 bytes raw, 2,569,377 compressed. | Refresh it once a day (and after game days). Take game-day injury news from `nflverse.json` or another lightweight source instead. Consider trimming player fields in the build step and serving a small `players.json` next to `nflverse.json`. |
| 1c | **The season-stats cache is never pruned.** Each player opened (free agents, head-to-head) adds a `sleeper_stats_v2_player_*` entry to localStorage. The player cache is already about 1.2 MB of the ~5 MB quota. Once it's full, writes fail silently and caching stops. | **Tested:** the cache sizes. The only `removeItem` calls are for old player-cache versions. Quota exhaustion is **inferred**. | Cap the entries (e.g. keep the newest ~150 by timestamp), drop entries from other seasons, and on a quota error evict the oldest and retry. |
| 1d | **Slot eligibility uses only `position`.** Sleeper also has `fantasy_positions` (multi-position players), which the optimizer and swap panel ignore. | **Inferred** from the code. Not checked whether any rostered players currently have more than one. | Include `fantasy_positions` in `PLAYER_FIELDS` and use it in `groupForSlot` / eligibility checks. Verify against live data how many players are affected. |

## Priority 3 — improvements

1. **Reliability:**
   - `fetchLeagueBundle` uses `Promise.all`, so one failed request (league/rosters/users/state) fails the whole load.
   - A first-time visitor gets nothing if the player-database download fails.
   - Six silent `catch(e){}` blocks hide errors.
2. **Maintainability:**
   - `index.html` is 3,780 lines / 249 KB.
   - The `cand-card` markup is built in 4 separate places.
   - The daylight-saving and practice-day rules are duplicated in `build_nflverse.py`.
   - There are no automated tests for scoring, the optimizer or the head-to-head direction rules.
3. **iPhone clutter:** waiver rows carry name, meta, "vs" line, snap, target/air share, projection and a VS button, and long names truncate next to chips.
4. **Head-to-head across positions:** "Better in N" counts one player's stats against the other's zeros (e.g. RB vs TE).
5. **Cold start:** the app doesn't remember the last user or league.

## What passed today (iPhone size, real data)

- All 7 Waivers filters show 15 rows each, sorted by projection.
- No script errors.
