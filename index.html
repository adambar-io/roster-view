Lineup cards

Compact shows what you need to set a lineup: projection, last game, opponent colored by matchup (green easy, amber average, red tough), injury or bye flags, and an arrow when snap share jumped or dropped. Tap a player for season stats, snaps, depth chart and the full matchup ranking. Detailed puts all of it on the card.

About sleepa

Read only. Lineup changes here are a plan; nothing is sent to Sleeper. Stats and projections come from Sleeper, scored with your league's settings. Opponents, snap share, target share and depth charts come from nflverse.

sleepa is an unofficial fan-made companion and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper.
